Frequently asked questions.

What the prototype checks and how to interpret its results.

Does PhishGuard open the URL I submit?

No. The current scanner analyzes URL text and may perform an optional WHOIS lookup. It does not visit the submitted web page or follow redirects.

Does a low score mean the website is safe?

No. It means few of the configured rules were triggered. Unknown attack patterns can be missed, and legitimate sites can also trigger warnings.

Is the score a probability?

No. The score is a transparent sum of rule-based indicator points. It is not calibrated against a representative real-world dataset.

Why is the ML output marked experimental?

The bundled demo dataset has known duplicate rows and a URL-scheme label proxy. The resulting model confidence should not be treated as a trustworthy real-world probability.

What does domain age mean?

It is an optional WHOIS-based lookup when the library and network are available. If the lookup fails, the result says unavailable; no age is guessed.

Can I submit a password-protected URL?

Do not submit URLs containing session tokens, private query parameters, credentials, or other sensitive information. Scan only public URL strings.
