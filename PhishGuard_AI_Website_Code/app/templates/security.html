SAFER BROWSING

Security guidance.

Use URL analysis as one signal in a broader verification process.

01

Verify the real domain

Read the hostname from right to left before the first path slash. Attackers may place trusted brand names in a subdomain or path.

02

Do not trust HTTPS alone

HTTPS encrypts traffic but does not guarantee that a site is operated by a legitimate organization.

03

Navigate independently

For banking, email, payments, and government services, use a saved bookmark or official app rather than an unexpected message link.

04

Protect credentials

Never share OTPs or passwords after following an unexpected link. Use multi-factor authentication and unique passwords.

05

Slow down

Urgent threats, surprise prizes, delivery fees, and account-lock warnings are common social-engineering tactics.

06

Report suspicious messages

Use the platform's reporting flow or contact the organization through independently verified channels.

!
Prototype boundary

PhishGuard does not fetch the submitted website or inspect its page contents. It cannot confirm live reputation, certificate validity, redirects, or malware payloads.
